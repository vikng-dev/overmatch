# Codex design review of the wall model (gpt-6.1-sol, read-only), 2026-10-30

Subject: the game's environment destruction, game state only. Prompt: the four lenses (stand-ins, tuning as a missing factor, realism, coherence). Reviewed main at fc9eb94; #188 by its issue only.

**Verdict:** Overmatch’s game-state destruction has a coherent architecture, but its mechanical laws do not yet form a coherent whole: intercepted blast energy becomes crushing work, transported fragment potency exceeds its energy source, and connectivity substitutes for structural capacity. Keep the shared chunk-and-joint architecture, but change those laws before treating the prototype’s outcomes as physical validation; the weight and ejecta additions promised in [the game slice](https://github.com/vikng-dev/overmatch/issues/188) are necessary but insufficient. I reviewed that slice’s contract, not its unseen implementation, and performed only source inspection and arithmetic.

**Findings, ranked by consequence**

1. **Fragment potency cannot serve as the wall’s physical energy budget. — Lenses: tuning, coherence.**  
   [spall_cone.ron:75](../../../assets/ballistics/spall_cone.ron:75), [spall_cone.rs:425](../../../src/ballistics/spall_cone.rs:425). The burst field emits **DERIVED 105 times** its driven casing energy: the authored **ASSUMED factor 210**, multiplied by the field’s emitted half. Recomputing the APHE fixture from its authored cavity and material inputs gives **DERIVED 211.1 kJ** driven, but **22.16 MJ** emitted; its **CLAIMED 0.060 kg** filler supplies only **DERIVED 0.251 MJ**, even under the prototype’s **ASSUMED TNT-equivalent chemistry**. This is an explicit fitted potency, not missing explosive energy.  
   **Law change:** transport actual fragment mass, velocity and kinetic energy; derive penetration and deposited energy from them. Keep HP susceptibility in receiver state and response, with the single energy-to-HP conversion. Do not feed the fitted field strength into brick crushing; account explicitly for the casing energy removed from the blast budget.

2. **The blast law confuses interception, impulse and work. — Lenses: stand-ins, tuning, realism, coherence.**  
   [wall.rs:363](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:363), [model.rs:535](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:535), [wall.rs:1207](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1207). Every intercepted ray spends its entire energy crushing brick, while impulse comes from `√(2 × filler mass × remaining energy)`. A rigid reflecting wall can receive substantial impulse while doing negligible mechanical work: solid angle alone cannot determine either quantity. Swept air’s outward scalar momentum is also not conserved as if it were an isolated projectile; the claim that reflection merely supplies a bounded correction to product momentum is unsupported.  
   **Law change:** use **CLAIMED Kinney–Graham/UFC pressure–time loading**, with **CLAIMED Hopkinson–Cranz scaled distance**, incidence, reflection and clearing. Apply traction to the wall; derive work from displacement and impulse from time integration. Use a separate confined-product/contact response near the charge. Rays remain useful visibility quadrature, but intercepted chemical energy is not deposited crushing work. Geometry alone does correctly approach hemispherical interception at contact; that does not establish mechanical coupling.

3. **A path to ground is not sufficient support, and the section search misses house failure mechanisms. — Lenses: stand-ins, realism, coherence.**  
   [model.rs:453](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:453), [model.rs:594](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:594), [model.rs:650](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:650). Connected matter remains standing regardless of the remaining path’s capacity. Structural candidates are individual joints or coplanar joint sets, and a candidate is examined only when removing it disconnects matter from ground. A wall attached through its corners and roof can require a combined base-and-side failure surface; none of its separate planes need disconnect it. The sandbox deliberately drops those house connections at [baked.rs:99](../../../src/sandbox/wall/baked.rs:99).  
   **Law change:** distribute gravity and applied loads through a reduced structural network, fail overloaded connections, redistribute, then use connectivity to identify detached groups. Permit combined failure surfaces or connection-level capacity failure. A reduced beam/panel or rigid-block model is sufficient; full finite-element physics is not owed.

4. **Partial crushing currently produces neither the perforation nor the failure shape. — Lenses: stand-ins, realism.**  
   [model.rs:340](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:340), [fracture.rs:102](../../../tools/src/buildings/fracture.rs:102), [house_1.building.ron:45](../../../assets/buildings/house_1/house_1.building.ron:45). The AP fixture deposits **MEASURED 278,002 J**. Using the prototype’s **ASSUMED crush-work model**, I derive **135.46 MJ/m³**, giving **0.002052 m³** removed, or a **51.1 mm** cylindrical radius through the **ASSUMED 0.25 m** wall. The sampled baked chunk has **ASSUMED volume 0.014625 m³**, so its pool is **DERIVED 1.981 MJ**: the hit spends only **14.0%**, leaving its entire collider present. Whole-depth chunks also cannot express a rear scab while retaining the front.  
   **Law change:** represent removed geometry separately from surviving chunk integrity, using local refinement or clipped crater/channel geometry, and provide depth layers for rear-face separation. Repeated shots through an existing channel must encounter reduced matter. Lowering the chunk-removal threshold would replace the missing geometry with an arbitrary hole-size multiplier.

5. **Brick strength incorrectly scales the inertia of displaced material. — Lenses: tuning, realism.**  
   [mechanism.rs:210](../../../src/ballistics/mechanism.rs:210), [ballistics.rs:258](../../../src/ballistics.rs:258), [materials.ron:252](../../../assets/materials/materials.ron:252). The strength ratio multiplies both the static resistance and velocity-squared terms. With the **ASSUMED factor 36.5/1000** and reference density **ASSUMED 7850 kg/m³**, brick consequently has **DERIVED effective inertial density 286.5 kg/m³**, against **CLAIMED 1845 kg/m³**. Holding the current static term and nose law fixed, the fixture’s **CLAIMED mass 10.2 kg**, **CLAIMED calibre 0.088 m**, **ASSUMED speed 773 m/s** and **ASSUMED chord 0.25 m** give **DERIVED losses 278 kJ versus 642 kJ** when only that density treatment changes. The latter is a sensitivity calculation, not a validated replacement prediction.  
   **Law change:** use independent material resistance and inertia terms, such as `F = A[R_material + B N ρ_material v²]`, within the chosen cavity law’s valid regime. Add porosity, confinement and finite-thickness response where needed; a strength factor cannot supply those effects.

6. **Seam fracture, rocking and toppling must be separate outcomes. — Lenses: realism, coherence.**  
   [model.rs:629](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:629), [wall.rs:1276](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1276). The prototype already includes mass through inertia; its omission is gravity and constrained motion. For the earlier **ASSUMED rectangular wall 4 × 2 × 0.25 m**, **CLAIMED density 1845 kg/m³**, **CLAIMED fracture energy 10 J/m²**, and **ASSUMED gravity 9.81 m/s²**, I derive **3690 kg**, **10 J** base-fracture work and **281.7 J** gravitational work to reach tipping balance about a bottom edge. The recorded AP angular impulse, **MEASURED 406 N·s·m**, supplies only **DERIVED 16.5 J** under that rigid-pivot idealization. A cracked base can therefore rock and remain standing.  
   **Law change:** determine fracture from connection traction and cohesive work; determine rocking/toppling from remaining kinetic energy, gravity, unilateral contact and friction. Do not add the tipping barrier to the seam-fracture threshold itself. The **MEASURED 1577 N·s·m** contact-blast case likewise does not establish toppling after this correction: its corresponding **DERIVED rotational energy is 248.8 J** under the same idealization.

7. **The same instantaneous impulse test cannot carry sustained pushing. — Lenses: stand-ins, tuning, realism, coherence.**  
   [model.rs:663](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:663), [trees.rs:927](../../../src/trees.rs:927). The tree precursor forgets subthreshold ticks and explicitly calibrates against a solver impulse reading. For an **ASSUMED constant 20 kN** contact at an **ASSUMED 1 m** lever, per-step angular impulse is **DERIVED 312.5 N·s·m at 64 Hz**, but **156.25 at 128 Hz**. A steady force must not become less capable merely because the timestep shrinks.  
   **Law change:** evolve structural displacement/rotation under force and torque, including stiffness, damping and gravity; an impulse approximation is valid only for loads short relative to structural response time. Read transferred contact impulse once, without solver accumulation artifacts. Apply its opposing momentum to the tank and carry the same response into rubble contact.

8. **The local Lamé field is a limited approximation being used as a universal load. — Lenses: stand-ins, realism.**  
   [model.rs:384](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:384), [model.rs:412](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:412), [wall.rs:1189](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1189). **CLAIMED Lamé cavity stresses** assume a pressurized cavity in a surrounding continuum. A surface blast is normal traction, not an embedded spherical cavity. The implementation also superposes sources without travel time, free-surface reflection or interruption by existing separations. From **CLAIMED modulus 8.6 GPa** and **CLAIMED density 1845 kg/m³**, through-thickness transit is **DERIVED 0.116 ms**; the fixture’s wall crossing takes approximately **DERIVED 0.331 ms**. Boundary effects cannot be assumed late or negligible.  
   **Law change:** retain the cavity approximation for sufficiently embedded, local kinetic response, with a stated validity domain. Use boundary-aware transient loading or a reduced plate/panel response for face loading and rear scabbing. Sources separated by gaps or disconnected material must not communicate through an unbroken infinite continuum.

9. **Connection properties mix distinct failure mechanisms and cannot yet carry trees. — Lenses: tuning, realism, coherence.**  
   [wall.rs:78](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:78), [model.rs:441](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:441), [model.rs:683](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:683), [contact.rs:103](../../../tools/src/buildings/contact.rs:103). Flexural masonry strength, interface opening energy and brick crushing resistance are not interchangeable handbook properties. Every geometric contact becomes a bond; mixed-material response is selected from whichever chunk is `a`. Shear, compression-dependent friction, bearing and timber grain are absent. Using **CLAIMED E = 8.6 GPa**, **f_t = 0.69 MPa** and **G_f = 10 J/m²** gives **DERIVED characteristic length E G_f/f_t² = 0.181 m**, comparable with the cut: agreement with a Griffith calculation using chunk size as flaw size does not establish mesh independence.  
   **Law change:** use a symmetric connection law with opening, shear/friction, compression and cohesive softening. Distinguish bonded attachment from bearing contact and include grain orientation for wood. Effective masonry can remain one material—separate mortar entities are unnecessary—but artificial cut size must not define its physical flaws. **CLAIMED Griffith and Mohr–Coulomb relations** provide relevant checks.

10. **The ejecta momentum share is underdetermined, and the handoffs do not conserve a complete budget. — Lenses: tuning, coherence.**  
    [wall.rs:1157](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1157), [wall.rs:1259](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1259), [wall.rs:1269](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1269). All lost energy wears the crushing pool, while that same loss powers cracks and an outgoing cone. Dust removes its assigned push; surviving groups receive unspent impulse after fracture. The AP fixture gives **DERIVED lost momentum 368.2 N·s**. Its nominal crushed mass is **DERIVED 3.786 kg**; giving that mass the entire lost forward momentum would require only **DERIVED 17.9 kJ**. Conservation alone therefore does not select the retained/ejected share.  
    **Law change:** maintain a coupled budget for crushing/compaction, fracture work, retained-body motion, ejecta motion and dissipated energy, with explicit ground reactions. Obtain ejecta velocity and mass from an unloading/ejection model, then subtract its vector momentum. **Owner-decision challenge:** exited-material strength alone cannot determine spall energy; derive that field from the same ejecta budget rather than another strength-scaled copy of deposited energy.

11. **Falling compounds have no route to crumble on landing or fracture again. — Lenses: stand-ins, realism, coherence.**  
    [model.rs:346](../../../src/sandbox/wall/model.rs:346), [wall.rs:1317](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1317), [wall.rs:1402](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1402). Fallen matter leaves the model’s standing state and becomes a permanent rigid compound. For the rectangle above, an **ASSUMED 1 m** centre-of-mass descent releases **DERIVED 36.2 kJ**—substantial beside its **DERIVED 10 J** base opening work. Permanent rigidity is visibly wrong for large masonry sections striking ground. The launch also uses a scalar inertia approximation rather than the compound’s tensor.  
    **Law change:** preserve surviving internal joints on detached bodies; feed landing contacts and later deposits back into their structural response, splitting compounds when connections fail. Initialize motion using the actual inertia tensor and remaining budget. I interpret the owner’s “no contact damage” decision as no HP dose to tanks; if it also forbids structural impact fracture, this is a merit-based challenge to that scope.

12. **Settlement freezes unsupported matter and can lose the first shove. — Lenses: stand-ins, coherence.**  
    [debris_stress.rs:433](../../../src/net/debris_stress.rs:433), [debris_stress.rs:491](../../../src/net/debris_stress.rs:491), [debris_stress.rs:501](../../../src/net/debris_stress.rs:501). The cap freezes moving pieces in mid-air. Resting pieces do not wake when supporting rubble moves, because rubble is excluded from the thaw trigger. Vehicle thaw runs after the physics step, so the initiating collision encounters infinite-mass static rubble before the piece becomes dynamic. Skipping vehicle-contacting pieces also means the cap is not a guaranteed upper bound.  
    **Law change:** freeze only supported equilibria; propagate waking through support dependencies and wake on blast, kinetic deposits and impending contact. Preserve the initiating contact’s momentum transfer. Meet budgets through admission control or compound/coarse representations, retaining their mass and occupied geometry; never manufacture airborne static supports.

13. **Two discretization/trigger shortcuts need bounded validity before reuse. — Lenses: stand-ins, tuning, realism.**  
    [wall.rs:97](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:97), [wall.rs:1206](../../../src/sandbox/wall.rs:1206), [fuze.rs:12](../../../src/rounds/fuze.rs:12), [88mm_HE.round.ron:21](../../../assets/rounds/88mm_HE.round.ron:21). With **ASSUMED 4096 rays**, an **ASSUMED 0.26 × 0.225 m** face at **ASSUMED 3 m** receives only **DERIVED approximately 2.12 rays** at normal incidence: adequate total interception does not establish adequate local cratering. Separately, the fuze compares whole-round resistance with a **CLAIMED approximately 30 N** internal spring threshold; the actuator’s moving mass/load path is missing, and fuze position is unused.  
    **Law change:** integrate visible face loading adaptively, preserving both resultant force and moment, with error bounded against response thresholds. For fuzes, model actuator response to acceleration/contact loading and pulse duration. “Any substantial solid triggers this nose fuze” can remain a restricted first-slice stand-in; it does not validate extrapolation to grazing contact, small debris or base fuzes.

**Sound as is**

- Offline convex pieces and exact face-overlap contacts are suitable geometric stand-ins; individual simulated bricks are unnecessary.
- Intact element colliders can replace their tiled chunks until the first deposit, provided that deposit is resolved against chunk data atomically.
- Connectivity correctly identifies detached groups **after** load-bearing connections have been assessed.
- Server-owned moving debris and replicated poses fit the shared presentation timeline; client debris physics would add disagreement without useful fidelity.
- Settled, supported rubble can become static once support-dependent waking is supplied. Dust needs presentation particles, not individual rigid bodies.

**Open questions for the owner**

- **Small-hole fidelity:** should a perforation smaller than a baked chunk pass both sight and later rounds? **Recommendation: yes.** Use local geometry refinement; a decal with an intact collider would expose the shortcut.
- **Rubble persistence and mobility:** may gameplay-relevant rubble disappear, and should tanks climb or push it? **Recommendation:** retain coarse occupied geometry throughout the match; let mass, friction, geometry and track contact determine movement. Fine visual debris may fade.
- **Contact-damage scope:** does the exclusion cover structural breakage on landing? **Recommendation:** exclude HP dose to tanks while allowing masonry connections to fracture under landing and pushing loads.
