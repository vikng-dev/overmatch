AUTHORED SCALE: 2 x 2 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [2000, 2000]` mm). Part of the pack's contract:
whatever binds it must repeat every 2 m, or every board renders at the wrong size. See
`building_surface::ROOF`. At 1024², that is 512 px/m.

THE SCATTER BUILDINGS' ROOFS — `building_surface::BuildingSurface` binds these three files, swapped
in on each building box's top face, the boards running along the box's longer horizontal side.
Shipped as KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 1024², cut from the pack's
2k masters by `scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh dark_planks`.
