Flipbook atlases generated in-repo (codex/image-gen, 2026-08-16; sources in

provenance. Same blast_atlas.py bake as above (source frames 0-11 into the 4x3 grid of 256 px
cells):
  blast_core_c.ktx2      — fireball_atlas_v1 (88 blast core, rounded cohesive blast)
  blast_core_d.ktx2      — fireball_atlas_v2 (88 blast core, asymmetric petaled silhouette)
  blast_core_e.ktx2      — fireball_atlas_v3 (88 blast core, compact spiked flash)
  blast_core_f.ktx2      — fireball_atlas_grey (88 blast core, v3's pre-greyed variant)

All are grayscale-with-alpha on purpose: the VFX materials recolor them through per-effect
gradient LUTs (src/vfx/billboard.rs), so one neutral sprite set serves every palette.
