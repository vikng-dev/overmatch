AUTHORED SCALE: 3 x 3 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [3000, 3000]` mm). Part of the pack's contract:
whatever binds it must repeat every 3 m, or every brick renders at the wrong size. `materials/visuals.ron`'s "brick" row
states it (`metres_per_repeat`). At 1024², that is 341 px/m.

THE CONSTRUCTIONS' PRIMARY PALETTE LANE — the standard half of `construction_surface::ConstructionMaterial`
binds these three files, on every face whose lane the construction data declares Primary. Shipped as KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd
supercompression, full mip chain) at 1024², cut from the pack's 2k masters by
`scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh brick_wall_006`.
