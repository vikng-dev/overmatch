AUTHORED SCALE: 2 x 2 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [2000, 2000]` mm). Part of the pack's contract:
whatever binds it must repeat every 2 m, or every board renders at the wrong size. `materials/visuals.ron`'s "dark_planks" row
states it (`metres_per_repeat`). At 1024², that is 512 px/m.

THE CONSTRUCTIONS' SECONDARY PALETTE LANE — `construction_surface::Surface` binds these three
files, on every face whose lane the construction data declares Secondary, the boards running along
the face's authored tangent (a graybox top's `top_tangent`, an element's surface frame).
Shipped as KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 1024², cut from the pack's
2k masters by `scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh dark_planks`.
