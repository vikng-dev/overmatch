Used for the track-link material `Mat_Track_Link` embedded in tiger_1.glb. Source pack

and packed into the material. Displacement is unused; the pack ships no ambient-occlusion map.

License note (2026-07-27)
-------------------------
The track-link material previously used FreePBR "worn-metal4", which is not CC0 and carried a
paid-license obligation. Those maps have been fully removed and replaced by the CC0 Metal046B
pack above. No FreePBR content remains anywhere in this repository:

  * the exported tiger_1.glb embeds only the Metal046B-derived payloads (verified by decoding
    every embedded image in the glb, not just by name);
  * the FreePBR image datablocks were deleted from the authoring file assets/tiger_1/tiger_1.blend;
  * git history and LFS are clean by construction -- the FreePBR-textured glb was never committed
    (the last committed revision of tiger_1.glb predates the track-link material entirely: it has
    15 materials, no Mat_Track_Link, and the Link primitive has neither UVs nor a material).

The remaining embedded textures in tiger_1.glb come from the CC-BY Sketchfab models credited
above.
