AUTHORED SCALE: 1.3 x 1.3 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [1300, 1300]` mm) — the smallest of the three
imported packs. Whatever binds it must tile at 1.3 m to keep its leaves and stones life-sized,
which repeats ~769x per km: this pack needs the blend slice's noise dithering more than the others,
or an explicit oversizing decision. At 2048², 1.3 m is 1575 px/m.

STAGED for the surface-blending slice — nothing loads this pack, so this folder carries its
contract and no binaries. `scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh brown_mud_leaves_01` cuts its three maps
as KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 2048², from Poly Haven 4k masters
that are not in the repo either. Poly Haven ships this pack's nor_gl / arm as 16-bit PNGs; the
encode script truncates them to 8-bit first (bit depth only, no colour-space change — channel
means match the source exactly).
