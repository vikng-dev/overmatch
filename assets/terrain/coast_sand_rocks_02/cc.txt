AUTHORED SCALE: 15 x 15 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [15000, 15000]` mm). This is part of the pack's
contract, not a preference: the world UVs must repeat every 15 m or every feature in the scan is
rendered at the wrong physical size. Declared as the base pack in `assets/terrain/surface.ron`.
At 4096² that is 273 px/m.

THE BASE PACK — `assets/terrain/surface.ron` names it, and the terrain binds these three files. Shipped as
KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 4096², built from the 4k masters by
scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh.
