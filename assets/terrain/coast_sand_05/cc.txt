AUTHORED SCALE: 25 x 25 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [24999.998, 25000.002]` mm — a scan fit, read
as 25 m on both axes). Whatever binds it must tile at 25 m to keep its sand and shell features
life-sized. At 2048², 25 m is 82 px/m.

STAGED for the surface-blending slice — nothing loads this pack, so this folder carries its
contract and no binaries. `scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh coast_sand_05` cuts its three maps as
KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 2048², from Poly Haven 4k masters that
are not in the repo either. Poly Haven ships this pack's nor_gl / arm as 16-bit PNGs; the encode
script truncates them to 8-bit first (bit depth only, no colour-space change).
