AUTHORED SCALE: 25 x 25 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [25000, 25000]` mm, confirmed by its own
`scale: "25M x 25M"` field) — the largest of the imported packs. Whatever binds it must tile at
25 m to keep its aerial dirt features life-sized. At 2048², 25 m is 82 px/m.

SHIPPED as slice 0 of the surface-blend arrays — the mask's recesses channel. This pack's
three maps are not files of their own: `scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh blend` cuts them, with
the other two layers, into `assets/terrain/blend/blend_*.ktx2` (KTX2 2D arrays, UASTC 4x4 +
zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 2048x2048 per slice, from Poly Haven 4k masters
that are not in the repo. See `assets/terrain/blend/cc.txt` for the slice order.
Poly Haven ships this pack's nor_gl / arm as 16-bit PNGs; the encode script truncates them to
8-bit first (bit depth only, no colour-space change).
