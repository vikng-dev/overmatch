AUTHORED SCALE: 8 x 8 m (Poly Haven `dimensions: [8000, 8000]` mm). Whatever binds it must tile at
8 m to keep its mud cracks and ruts life-sized. At 2048², 8 m is 256 px/m.
This pack's `scale: "40x40"` field disagrees with its `dimensions`; `dimensions` is the millimetre
field rocks_ground_02's own `scale` pins, and is what the contract above is read from.

SHIPPED as slice 2 of the surface-blend arrays — the mask's lowlands channel. This pack's
three maps are not files of their own: `scripts/encode-terrain-ktx2.sh blend` cuts them, with
the other two layers, into `assets/terrain/blend/blend_*.ktx2` (KTX2 2D arrays, UASTC 4x4 +
zstd supercompression, full mip chain) at 2048x2048 per slice, from Poly Haven 4k masters
that are not in the repo. See `assets/terrain/blend/cc.txt` for the slice order.
