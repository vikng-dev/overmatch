Camouflage patterns (shared, named by a livery's `camouflage.pattern`)
---------------------------------------------------------------------

"stripes" — generated in-project by `scripts/visuals/camo_stripes.py` (fixed seed); no external
source. A linear RGB mask: R the livery's first camouflage colour's coverage, G its second, B its
third (unused by this pattern); 0 where the base paint shows. Shipped as KTX2 (UASTC 4x4 + zstd,
full mip chain, linear) at 1024², tiling seamlessly in both directions, by
`scripts/encode-visuals-ktx2.sh stripes`.
